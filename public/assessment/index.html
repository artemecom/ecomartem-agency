AI Loop Assessment

A 5-point self-audit for Shopify DTC brands doing $1M–$10M/year. Find where your store leaks revenue because AI isn't in the loop yet.

How to use this: Go through each loop below. Be honest. If you can't answer "yes, it's automated" — that loop is open. Most $1M–$5M stores have 1–2 loops closed at best. The gaps are where your CAC is inflated and your ROAS is underperforming.
The 5 Revenue Loops
01
Ad Creative Loop
Are your top-performing creatives automatically feeding back into your creative briefs? Or is your team still guessing what to produce next based on gut feel?
Same 3–5 creatives running for 60+ days with no systematic refresh signal
02
Retention Trigger Loop
Does your email/SMS fire based on actual purchase behavior signals and predicted LTV — or fixed day-timers that treat a $20 buyer the same as a $400 buyer?
"Day 7 winback" flows not segmented by LTV or product category
03
Inventory–Ad Spend Loop
Does your ad spend automatically throttle down on SKUs going out of stock? Or do you burn budget sending traffic to sold-out PDPs?
Manually pausing ad sets when products run out — or not at all
04
COGS–ROAS Loop
Is your target ROAS calculated per-SKU based on actual margin — or one blended number across the entire catalog that makes low-margin products look profitable?
One ROAS target for products with $8 margin and $40 margin
05
Creative Fatigue Loop
Do you have an automated signal for when a creative is dying — before your ROAS visibly craters? Or are you catching it two weeks late after budget is already burned?
Checking dashboards manually every few days with no automated fatigue alerts
Score Yourself
0–1
Loops active — significant leak You're leaving 20–40% efficiency on the table. CAC is inflated, ROAS is suppressed. The fixes are systematic, not tactical.
2–3
Loops active — solid base, clear gaps Good foundation. The open loops are likely costing $5–15k/mo in wasted spend or missed retention revenue.
4–5
Loops active — scaling ready You're in the top 10% of operators. Now the question is what's next — let's find the ceiling.
