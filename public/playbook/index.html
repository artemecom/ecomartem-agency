01

The truth about ecom in 2026

Three shifts that change everything. Internalize these and you are ahead of 90% of people starting stores.

1. The product is commoditized.

Anyone can source the same product and spin up the same store with AI. “I found a winning product” is no longer an advantage. It is table stakes. The advantage is attention.

2. Paid ads are rent, organic is an asset.

Ads work, but the second you stop paying, the traffic stops. Organic content compounds: a good post keeps working for months and the next one stacks on top. Rich people own assets. Same rule, applied to attention.

3. Agentic commerce is coming fast.

Your customers are starting to ask AI agents “find me the best X under $50” and buying what the agent surfaces. In 12 months a big chunk of buyers will not visit your store at all. Stores built to be understood by both humans and agents win the next wave.

Stop obsessing over the product and the theme. Start obsessing over distribution.
02

Build the store fast, and right

Do not spend three weeks here. A clean, trustworthy store that converts, live in a day or two. Then get to the real work.

Pick the niche for distribution, not just margin

Before the product, ask: can I make interesting content about this every day for 6 months? A product you cannot make content about is a dead store no matter how good it is.

The setup that actually moves conversion

Store launch checklist
  • One clear promise above the fold (what, who for, why better)
  • Real product photos or video, not just supplier stock
  • 3+ reviews visible on the product page
  • Fast, obvious checkout: express pay on, mobile-first
  • A genuine reason to buy now (bundle, drop, shipping timeline)
  • Shipping and returns stated plainly
  • Pixel and analytics installed

What does not matter yet: a custom theme, a paid logo, 40 products. One hero product, one clean page, live. Refine after real traffic tells you what is wrong.

03

Nail the offer before you touch traffic

The most expensive mistake in ecom: pouring traffic onto an offer that does not convert. That is how I burned that budget to zero sales. The traffic was not the problem. The offer was not ready.

Your first 100 orders teach you the offer. Until you get sales from a little traffic, do not scale.

A leaky bucket does not need more water. It needs patching.

Offer template
For [who], [product] gives you [outcome] without [the pain]. Proof: [reviews / demo / numbers]. [Bundle / price] + [guarantee] + [reason to buy now].

If you cannot fill every line honestly, the offer is not ready. Fix it before Module 4.

04

The traffic system (the real 80%)

This is the whole game. Read it twice.

Rule 1: Organic first, one channel, go deep.

Pick the ONE platform where your buyer already scrolls. Five platforms done badly loses to one done well. Master one, then clone.

Rule 2: Content earns the view, it does not buy it.

An ad buys a guaranteed view and sells hard. Organic has to earn the view with a hook and hold it with retention, and if it smells like an ad, the algorithm kills the reach.

The organic formula
  • Hook first: stop the scroll in the first 1-2 seconds / first line
  • Retention before the product: lead with something worth watching
  • Native, not glossy: phone-shot energy beats studio-ad energy
  • CTA in the caption or comments, never a “SHOP NOW” hammer on the video

Rule 3: Cadence beats intensity.

3-5 pieces a week for 6 months beats one viral swing. Month 2 is quiet for everyone. The people who win just do not stop.

Hook bank · steal structures, not words
"Most [people in niche] do X. It is killing their [result]." "I spent [time/money] learning this so you do not have to." "Nobody talks about why [common thing] actually [surprising outcome]." "Stop doing [common advice]. Do this instead." "[Number] [things] that [outcome] (most miss #[n])."
05

Produce content daily without burnout

The traffic system only works if you can actually produce content every day. Most people quit here. This is how you do not.

Automate the hands. Keep the head.

Automate: research, first drafts, resizing, subtitles, variations, scheduling prep, turning your catalog into video. Never automate: the hook, the point of view, and the reply in the first hour. That is what makes a stranger trust you. A fully automated account gets flagged and dies. You post by hand, always.

The hard part: product video at volume

I stopped hand-making every clip. I connect my store catalog to a tool that turns my actual products into organic-style short videos, and it learns from what performs and feeds the next batch. It is called CartViral. It does not auto-post (a ban risk that kills reach). It generates the clips, I pick and post them myself, and the next wave gets smarter from the numbers. It gives me the volume so I can keep the judgment.

The weekly rhythm · steal it
  • Mon: pull trends, pick angles for the week (30 min)
  • Daily: 1 short video + 1 text post, posted by hand, reply in the first hour
  • 2x/week: 1 carousel
  • Fri: look at the numbers, keep what worked, cut what did not
06

Be visible to the AI agents too

Most store owners have not noticed this yet, which is exactly why it is an opening.

When a buyer asks an AI agent to shop for them, the agent reads your product data and your content. Rich, specific, consistent, and you show up. Thin supplier defaults, and you are invisible to the fastest-growing buyer online.

  • Write real, specific product descriptions (benefits + details), not supplier copy
  • Keep content and catalog consistent (same product framing everywhere)
  • Clean, structured product data

The same content that makes your clips also feeds agent visibility. One asset, two audiences: humans in the feed and agents doing the shopping.

07

Measure what actually matters

Vanity kills focus. I once had a post do 400k views and make me nothing, then a boring one made 40 grand. Reach is not revenue.

Track only these, weekly
  • Revenue and orders (the only truth)
  • Conversion rate on your product page (is the offer working)
  • Which content drove profile visits and link clicks (which angle sells)
  • Cost to acquire, even organic has a time cost
Motivation is a scam. A moving number is fuel. Never work more than a week without evidence.
